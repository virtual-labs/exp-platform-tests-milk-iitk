### About the Experiment 

In this experiment, platform tests of milk—Clot-on-Boiling (COB) and Methylene Blue Reduction Test (MBRT)—will be conducted to assess the preliminary quality of raw milk. The COB test detects developed acidity and heat stability, while the MBRT estimates microbial load based on dye reduction time. These rapid tests help in screening milk at the reception level, ensuring safety, preventing processing losses, and maintaining quality standards.
